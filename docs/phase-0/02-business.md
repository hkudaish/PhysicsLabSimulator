# Business and financial analysis

Source: BRD sections 1–12 and V2 addendum; financial workbook sheets Assumptions, Revenue, Operating P&L, Scenarios, Dashboard, Sources & Notes, Phase Funding V2. All figures below describe the supplied scenario, not actual revenue or financial advice.

Proposed segment: Saudi private schools and school groups, with GCC expansion later. Buyer, grade range, curriculum mapping, procurement cycle and willingness to pay remain unvalidated. Scientific lead approves model accuracy; instructional lead approves learning objectives; customer success owns onboarding/training/support; IT owns bulk installation; product owner resolves scope and gates.

Plans, prices, seats, allowed devices, content and grace periods are versioned administrative data, never scientific-core constants. Model subscriptions as draft → active → renewal-due → expired/cancelled; suspension is explicit, audited and distinct from data deletion. Honor issued offline allocations until their declared expiry. Renewal extends entitlements through a new audited term. Refunds, invoices, contracts, payments and recognized revenue are separate records; payment-provider integration waits for approved requirements.

Institution onboarding: approved agreement → create tenant/campus/lab → configure roles → register devices and preinstall approved content → allocate seats → train teachers → assign lessons → review usage/support → renew or export/delete under policy. Demo/pilot invitations or messages have not been sent.

Before spending decisions: owner/finance lead must approve an institution-only scenario, headcount and vendor quotes, budget overlap treatment, revenue recognition/collection assumptions, and pilot evidence.
